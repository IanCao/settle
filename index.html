Studionet

Most escrows can only answer "was it signed". This one answers "is it good enough".

Write acceptance criteria in plain words, lock funds, and the provider delivers a URL. Validators fetch the artifact and judge it against the criteria; the payout is a percentage - full, half or none. The client can contest with counter evidence, and the second round weighs both documents.

How it settles

  1. FUNDED - criteria pinned, clock running. No model yet.
  2. DELIVERED - the provider posted one artifact URL.
  3. JUDGED - validators read the artifact against the criteria: ACCEPT / PARTIAL / REJECT, payout 100% / 50% / 0%.
  4. DISPUTED - the client filed counter evidence inside the window. Round two is adversarial: both documents.
  5. SETTLED - the split is credited. Withdraw sends it out.

Deliver nothing by the deadline and refund_expired makes the client whole - that path never touches a model.
